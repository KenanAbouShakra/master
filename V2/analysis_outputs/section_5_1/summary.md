## Supported coverage facts

The six repositories are `docker/cli`, `prometheus/prometheus`, and `tektoncd/pipeline` for development (including training and temporal holdout), and `pytest-dev/pytest`, `helm/helm`, and `containerd/containerd` for external evaluation. The configured study window is 2025-06-23 through 2026-08-03, with 59 Monday-anchored weeks per repository (354 total). The audited panel confirms 36 training weeks per development repository (108), four purge weeks each (12), 19 holdout weeks each (57), and for each external repository 13 calibration plus 46 evaluation weeks (39 + 138). Thus RQ1's planned evaluation total is 57 + 138 = 195 repository-weeks. These are planned calendar weeks; measurement availability and detector evaluability are separate quantities in the CSVs.

The frozen metadata reports 123,028 rows in `analysis_outputs/attempt_measurements.csv.gz`. Direct inspection confirms 123,028 records and no duplicates by `(repo_full, run_id, attempt_number)`. The artifact is attempt-level records restricted to the six frozen repositories and configured PR-validation events (`pull_request`, `pull_request_target`, `merge_group`); not 123,028 logical runs or eligible outcomes. The builder joins job summaries on the same composite key and assigns a logical run to the week of the parent run's `created_at`. There is no separate deduplication step after that join; the unique composite key is verified on the stored output.

Measurement support counts are reconstructed from the audited repository-week panel. Non-null weekly latency, failure rate, and rerun rate count as available; attempt, eligible outcome, logical-run, rerun-logical-run, and additional-attempt totals are provided separately. Missing measurement values remain missing. The manifest's attempt coverage is a repository-level proportion of eligible event runs represented by at least one attempt record, not a weekly completeness fraction; it is 1.0 for four repos, 0.99904 for containerd, and 0.99974 for tektoncd.
